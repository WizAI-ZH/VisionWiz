## VisionWiz 1.8.2

### 中文

- 优化模型测试程序上传：已存在相同内容的 KModel 时自动复用，避免再次传输大文件；支持同内容不同名称的模型，并增加上传文件校验。
- 优化程序上传稳定性，改善部分上传失败和中断后的恢复流程。
- 图像采集新增实时 FPS、JPEG 85 极清档，并优化 224×224 等取景分辨率以及重新连接后的预览启动。
- 更新助手新增「重试／继续下载」「打开文件夹」「取消」「确定」按钮；关闭下载窗口后正确释放安装包，支持下次继续下载。
- 已通过实体威智板 USB 模型复用测试及本地回归测试。其他硬件与网络环境仍建议进一步验证。

### English

- Improved model-test uploads by reusing identical KModel files already on the device, even with different names, and verifying the transferred file contents.
- Improved program-upload stability and recovery after failed or interrupted transfers.
- Added actual preview FPS, a JPEG 85 highest-quality option, improved native framing for resolutions including 224×224, and more reliable preview startup after reconnecting.
- Added clickable Retry/Resume, Open Folder, Cancel, and OK buttons to the update helper. Closing an in-progress download releases its installer and retains resumable downloads.
- USB model reuse was verified on a physical controller alongside local regression tests. Other hardware and network environments may require further validation.
